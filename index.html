The problem

Colleges create data every day: admissions, attendance, marks, exam papers, lecture videos. Much of it sits on staff laptops, USB drives and one server in the admin office. A single disk failure, ransomware attack or lost laptop can wipe out a semester of work.

What went wrong before

  • No backup copy stored outside the building
  • All data on the same costly disks, old or new
  • Nobody knows how long recovery would take
  • Everyone can open everything

Match the storage to how the data is used

Data opened every minute needs fast disks. Data opened once a year can sit on cheap storage. Tiering keeps performance high and cost low. Select a tier to see details.

    Conclusion

    Good storage management is what lets a college trust its own records. Tiering controls cost, RAID and the 3-2-1 backup rule protect against failure, and clear access rules protect student privacy.
